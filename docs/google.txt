Google LLC is an American multinational corporation and technology company focusing on online advertising, search engine technology, cloud computing, computer software, quantum computing, e-commerce, consumer electronics, and artificial intelligence (AI).

Google was founded on September 4, 1998, by Larry Page and Sergey Brin while they were PhD students at Stanford University in California. Its parent company is Alphabet Inc., created in 2015 during a corporate restructuring.

Google developed the Gemini family of multimodal AI models, as well as the Android operating system, Chrome web browser, and Google Cloud Platform.
