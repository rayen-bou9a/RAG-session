Nvidia Corporation is an American technology company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. It was founded in April 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem.

NVIDIA's first graphics accelerator was called the NV1, released in 1995. It combined 2D/3D graphics, audio, and a Sega Saturn gamepad port on a single PCI card.

To enter the mobile processor and baseband market, NVIDIA acquired Icera in May 2011 for $367 million. Today, NVIDIA is best known for its GeForce GPUs, CUDA parallel computing platform, and AI data center accelerators.
